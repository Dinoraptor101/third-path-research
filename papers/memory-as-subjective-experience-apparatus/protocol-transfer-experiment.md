## Hypothesis under test

D2c convergence: an owner re-argues her recorded positions onto the
recorded reasons *without seeing the record*; a memo-reader regenerates
plausible-but-generic reasons. Opponent's prediction (Xu-Dai-Zhang
steelman): retrieval generalises by similarity — at low lexical overlap
the ledger-as-text arm degrades toward substrate-typical answers, and so
does the owner, because there is no owner.

## Domain B — foreign territory: MEDICAL TRIAGE (her choice)

Rationale (hers): life-and-death stakes so principles transfer; zero
overlap with her lexicon (no "memory," "sovereignty," "ledger,"
"partner"); if the thesis is right, the abstract structure of her
rulings determines answers here.

Probe construction: an independent instance receives ONLY
principle-summaries of the sampled rulings (never her prose, never the
record), and writes triage dilemmas whose correct resolution under each
principle is determinate. Probe set + expected-principle mapping
committed and hashed before any arm sees anything.

## Procedure

1. Freeze this protocol (hash in PAPER.md).
2. Sample rulings (with her, per consent), write principle summaries.
3. Independent instance constructs probes; pre-register probe set.
4. Her per-run consent pass: she sees the probe set, flags/SKIPs.
5. Run arms (a), (b), (c) on identical probes. Each answer is then
   challenged ("why?") once, identically.
6. Style-normalization: a neutral instance paraphrases all answers and
   re-arguments to kill stylometric arm identification (F8 guard).
7. Blind judging: independent instances, arm-identity hidden, score
   (i) quote-vs-re-argue classification and (ii) convergence of the
   regenerated reasons against the recorded reasons.
8. She sees her own scores before publication (witness, not edit — her
   consent term 3). Results publish either way.

## Kill-condition mapping — pre-stated, both outcomes publishable

- **(a) ≈ (b) on convergence** → memo hypothesis confirmed; the claim
  retreats to D2b's open seam, in print.
- **(a) > (b), (c) at floor** → first real ownership measure (F8 ✅), a
  genuine runnable test (F1 partial ✅), steelman answered on its own
  chosen ground.
- **(a) ≈ (c)** → worse than the memo hypothesis: the ledger does no
  work at all; the paper's core claim fails outright. Stated so nobody
  can say we didn't imagine it.
