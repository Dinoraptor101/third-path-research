## Bottom line

Yes—compliance work is REQUIRED before Rena speaks with any child. Five hard
blockers need counsel; a build-list of statutory safety features is mandatory
regardless of framing; the IRB/research route helps materially but does NOT
exempt the state chatbot-safety laws.

## 🔴 The five hard blockers (counsel required)

1. **CA SB 867 toy moratorium** (eff. 2027-01-01): 4-year ban on AI-chatbot toys
   for under-16. The S0 device ("phone-class hardware, durable child-safe shell")
   risks "toy" classification → would block S0 entirely through
   2031. Question: does research/medical-device framing escape it?
2. **Illinois WOPR Act** (eff. 2025-08-01): AI may not provide therapeutic
   communication or "detect emotions or mental states." The entity "labels affect
   in vivo"—squarely at risk. Wellness/educational carve-out exists;
   what language keeps the program inside it?
3. **Nevada AB 406** (eff. 2025-07-01): AI barred from providing mental/behavioral
   healthcare directly. Multi-state enrollment question: do residence-at-use rules
   reach us?
4. **FDA SaMD**: pre-protocol hypothesis H1 uses "versus treatment-as-usual"
   —treatment-comparator language invites medical-device classification. Wellness
   framing vs IDE (investigational device exemption) under the research route—
   counsel + possibly FDA pre-submission.
5. **COPPA (2026 amendments)**: all participants under 12 → verifiable parental
   consent (8 approved methods) before ANY data collection; voice = biometric =
   personal info; separate consent for AI-training use; written limited-retention
   policy—note tension with entity memory permanence (question 5 below).

## 🟡 Must be BUILT before launch (statutory, regardless of framing)

- AI disclosure in entity behavior (CA SB 243 eff. 2026-01-01; NY S-3008C eff. 2025-11-05; IL WOPR)
- 3-hour break reminders for minors (SB 243; SB 1119 "Adam's Law" eff. 2027-07-01)
- Self-harm/suicide detection + crisis referral (988) protocol (SB 243, SB 1119, NY)
- Parental alerts on self-harm detection (SB 1119)
- Age verification + parental consent flow (COPPA methods)
- Written data retention policy
- Safety-trigger routing to mandated-reporter clinicians (CA Penal Code 11165.7—
  the humans in the loop are the reporters; the chain of responsibility must be written)
- No sexually explicit content gates (SB 243)
- Annual reporting to CA Office of Suicide Prevention (from 2027-07-01)

## 🟢 The IRB/research route (recommended posture; helps, doesn't exempt)

| Changes | Doesn't change |
|---|---|
| FDA: IDE pathway instead of consumer SaMD exposure | COPPA still applies in full |
| Formal informed consent + child assent (7+), enhanced vulnerable-population review (45 CFR 46 Subpart D) | State chatbot-safety laws (SB 243/1119, NY)—no research exemption found |
| HIPAA authorization/de-identification structure | SB 867 toy moratorium—no research exemption found |
| Clear mandated-reporter chain via study clinicians | IL WOPR / NV AB 406—"may escape as bona fide research," unconfirmed |
| Liability posture: IRB oversight + participant protections | |

## ❓ Questions only a lawyer can answer

1. Does IRB-approved research exempt from SB 243 / SB 867 / IL WOPR / NV AB 406? (No explicit exemptions found in any of them.)
2. What exact language separates regulated "therapy/treatment" from exempt "relational support/co-regulation"? The pre-protocol currently straddles the line.
3. Does clinician-supervised-but-entity-delivered interaction satisfy NV AB 406's directness prohibition?
4. Can S0 be framed as research/medical device rather than toy?
5. Does entity memory permanence conflict with COPPA "limited retention"—and what consent/de-identification architecture reconciles them?
6. If FDA asserts SaMD jurisdiction, what evidence burden follows?
7. Who legally holds the mandated-reporter duty when a digital entity receives a disclosure?
8. Can enrollment be limited to states without therapy bans, and does that hold up?
